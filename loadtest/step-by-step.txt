What the report shows
Board ops: how many were delivered out of how many should have been, plus p50/p95/p99 latency.
Cursors and drafts: loss rate, latency, and how many drafts were too big to send at all. The size limit is read from the connection, not guessed.
PASS/FAIL targets: everyone connected, ops delivered ≥ 99.5%, op p95 ≤ 250 ms, datagram loss ≤ 5%.
Load generator health: if the bots' own processes fell behind, the report says so, because that inflates every latency. Your mentor is likely to ask about this.
Convergence (contention scenario only): after the fight over shared shapes, it checks each client's view against the server's final state. It does this twice: once the way the frontend applies updates today (whatever arrives last wins), and once with a timestamp check. I expect the frontend version to show some clients ending up with the wrong position or size, but that hasn't been measured yet.
